02 Independent projects

VEE AI Software

I'm exploring how software and AI could make everyday work easier for industrial parts distributors.

What I'm exploring

A simple parts enquiry can involve searching for product details, checking specifications, finding stock and preparing a quote. I'm interested in how those steps fit together and where software could help.

How I'm approaching it

I want to understand a problem before trying to automate it. That means looking at how the work is done, building small prototypes and testing whether an idea is actually useful. QuoteDesk is one example of that approach.

Next build

VEE DataDesk

My next idea focuses on the product information behind a quote.

Product records aren't always complete or consistent. With DataDesk, I want to explore how AI could help organise that information, spot missing specifications and give staff a chance to review changes before saving them.
