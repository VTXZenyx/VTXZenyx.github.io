2026

Making technical enquiries and quotations easier to manage. This is a working local software prototype I've been developing through VEE AI Software, inspired by how industrial parts distributors manage technical customer requests.

The application keeps enquiries, possible catalogue matches, missing specifications, clarification questions, simulated stock and pricing, and quotation approval in one workspace.

I've also worked on quotation revisions, database reliability, technical checks and testing. One thing I've focused on is keeping important decisions with the employee rather than letting software invent technical details or approve unsupported changes.

2026

Exploring how AI, data and risk management work together in financial markets. My interest in cryptocurrency and market behaviour has grown into an independent quantitative trading and software engineering project.
