I'm a second-year student at Victoria University of Wellington studying Information Systems, Digital Intelligence and Data Science.

I've always liked technology, but what interests me most is understanding how it can actually be useful. I enjoy working with data, figuring out how systems work, solving problems and learning how technology connects with people and businesses.

I genuinely enjoy coding, especially working with R, Python and SQL. I'm still learning and I know there is a lot more for me to learn, which is something I actually enjoy. I like trying new things, building projects, making mistakes and gradually getting better at them.
