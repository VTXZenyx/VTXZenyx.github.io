02 Industry research

VEE AI Software

Before I write software for a distributor, I want to understand what happens when someone phones up looking for a part.

Conversations with distributors

As part of this independent student project, I've reached out to about 24 industrial businesses across New Zealand. My research conversations have included ECKO Fastening Systems, ATS Pneumatics and SealLAB.

Questions I'm asking

How do staff identify a part from an incomplete enquiry? Where do they look for its specifications, price and stock level? I'm following those steps through to the quote and asking where staff have to enter the same details twice.

What's next

VEE DataDesk

My next build is about the product record behind the quote.

I want to explore how AI could help staff turn supplier information into consistent product records. I'd start by flagging missing specifications so an employee can check them before saving the record.
